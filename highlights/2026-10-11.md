## 1. Agent Plasticity: Measuring Self-Improvement Through Experience

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08902)

- **Published:** 2026-10-06
- **Type / source:** Paper · Harman Singh et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce agent plasticity to measure held-out performance gains per learning cost as agents accumulate reusable artifacts. They find that endpoint capability and improvement efficiency diverge, and that training gains transfer only partly out of distribution.

## 4. AutoSciBench: Autonomous Benchmark Generation for Evaluating Scientific Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05140)

- **Published:** 2026-10-04
- **Type / source:** Paper · Dongki Kim et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors adapt scientific benchmarks using solver trajectories and judge feedback. They report generated tasks reducing solver accuracy by 22.4 and 25.5 percentage points in computational biology and materials science.
