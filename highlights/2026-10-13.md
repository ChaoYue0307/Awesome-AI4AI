## 2. Proactive Agentic Orchestration

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13485)

- **Published:** 2026-10-09
- **Type / source:** Paper · Xueyang Feng et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors release POrchestra, allowing sub-agents to request harness adjustments during execution, and OPOD, which distills hindsight feedback into orchestration training. Across three long-horizon benchmarks, they report average relative success gains of 9.1% over the strongest orchestration baseline and 10.0% over existing small-orchestrator training baselines.

## 3. Memento 3: Model-Based Recursive Self-Improvement through Reflective Rulebooks

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11794)

- **Published:** 2026-10-08
- **Type / source:** Paper · Haoyu Zhao et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors keep the LLM fixed while revising persistent natural-language world models, compiling them into code and verifying observed transitions. They report clearing all levels of 25 public ARC-AGI-3 games with mean RHAE 100.0 using 44% of the human action count.

## 6. Complex Agents, Shallow Tests: Demystifying and Enhancing Test Adequacy of Agent Harness in the Wild

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04921)

- **Published:** 2026-10-04
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yifan Xiong et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce HarnessTester, which generates tests grounded in agent-harness contracts. They report detecting 122 real-world harness bugs, including 88 previously unknown bugs and 69 confirmed by developers, alongside larger coverage and mutation-score gains than general-purpose test generators.

## 7. MESH-Harness: Self-Improving Agent Harnesses via Bandit-Guided Compositional Evolution

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05300)

- **Published:** 2026-10-04
- **Type / source:** Paper · Zhiwei Shang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors combine modular harness reuse with bandit-guided compositional search while fixing model weights. Under matched candidate-evaluation budgets, they report gains of 2.00 to 7.01 points over Meta-Harness across four domains and 14.6% lower total cost including search.

## 8. Safety Must Survive Self-Improvement: Why Failures Persist and How Agents Recover

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01073)

- **Published:** 2026-10-01
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yunbei Zhang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** In a controlled authorization testbed, the authors find that stale scores preserve unsafe agents in 22 of 48 framework histories after requirements change. Full validation and validated rollback yield fully correct programs in the core study while retaining over 43% deployment savings.

## 10. SoL-Pi: Recursively Scaling Auto-Research Loops for Efficient Agent Harness

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20519)

- **Published:** 2026-09-17
- **Type / source:** Paper · Haozhe Liu et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors release SoL-Pi after auto-research selects reusable mechanisms for execution, compaction, observations and delegated reading. On 51 EdgeBench tasks with two model backbones, they report performance comparable to Pi while reducing recorded token traffic by 44.7-49.0% and API cost by about one third.
