## 3. Recursive Reward Improvement via Adversarial Meta-Prompt Evolution

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21040)

- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Type / source:** Paper · Wei Dai et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors use reward-hacking adversaries to repair generated rubrics before solver training and improve future task generation. On HealthBench Professional, RRI raises adjusted scores over the Generation Only baseline by 10.9 points at 9B and 12.4 at 27B; the 27B model also transfers to two unseen professional benchmarks.

## 4. Doc2Harness: Benchmarking LLMs on Long-Horizon Harness Engineering of Coding Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20344)

- **Published:** 2026-10-14
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yuyao Ge et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors benchmark harness construction from interface skeletons and development documents, then freeze each harness and test it in isolated containers using a scripted mock model. Across 19 models, three builds each and 328 test cases, the best pass rate is 53.05%, exposing substantial harness-engineering failures.

## 5. A Self-Improving Agent that Grows its Own DSL: Oracle-Free Operator Induction for Exact Question Answering

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20809)

- **Published:** 2026-10-14
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yun-Shiuan Chuang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors grow an exact domain-specific language with a frozen model, admitting operators only when independent implementations agree and conservation laws hold. In the evaluated bounded domains, the gate rejects all 138 buggy operator variants and transfers to business-ledger and clinical-encounter schemas without a reward model or answer key.

## 6. Verifier-Harness Co-Evolution for Training-Free Self-Improvement on Open-Ended Tasks

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17609)

- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Type / source:** Paper · Tianyi Xu et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors evolve an executable verifier together with persistent harness state while keeping model parameters fixed. UCoE selects verifiers using controlled response contrasts; on six open-ended benchmarks it achieves 59.00% and 57.19% preference against matched direct generation with two Qwen backbones, with code linked by the paper.

## 7. Drafting Agents, Not Just Tokens: Speculative Recursive Self-Improvement for Agentic Systems

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16491)

- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Type / source:** Paper · Xin Xu et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors draft executable successor agents before predecessors finish evaluation but require complete verification before adoption. SpecRSI allocates search and checking budgets jointly; it reports coding-validation success of 69.4% versus HGM's 64.3% at a 20.8% lower target-cost index, with code linked by the paper.
