## 1. Beam is now available

## 2. Self-Evolving Coding Agents via Harness-Policy Optimization

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22791)

- **Published:** 2026-10-16
- **Type / source:** Paper · Zhepei Wei et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce Self-HPO, where the evolving policy proposes its own harness and then learns under that harness with reinforcement learning. On three coding benchmarks, they report average resolve rate rising from 57.8% to 64.8%; code, harnesses and checkpoints are released.

## 3. Recursive Reward Improvement via Adversarial Meta-Prompt Evolution

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21040)

- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Type / source:** Paper · Wei Dai et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors repair generated rubrics using reward-hacking adversaries before training the solver and use those exploits to improve future task generation. On HealthBench Professional they report gains of 10.9 and 12.4 adjusted-score points at 9B and 27B, with transfer to unseen professional tasks.

## 4. Doc2Harness: Benchmarking LLMs on Long-Horizon Harness Engineering of Coding Agents

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20344)

- **Published:** 2026-10-14
- **Type / source:** Paper · Yuyao Ge et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors evaluate harness engineering with interface contracts and development documents, then freeze candidate harnesses and test them using a scripted mock model in isolated containers. Across 19 models, three builds each and 328 test cases, the highest reported pass rate is 53.05%.

## 5. SEAR: Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18792)

- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Type / source:** Paper · Xinyi Wang et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors release a robotics framework and benchmark spanning code-as-policy, agent-as-policy and VLA auto-research. Their controls distinguish retained learning from more attempts, budget differences, familiar tasks and privileged state across 330 simulated and five physical tasks.

## 7. Harness^2: Recursive Agent Harnessing for an Open World

[**Read source →**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224)

- **Published:** 2026-10-09
- **Type / source:** Paper · Ruiyao Xu et al. · arXiv
- **Why it matters:** The authors introduce Harness^2 with task-level Propose-Probe-Compose edits and a continual improvement harness that carries experience to future tasks. Without ground-truth verifiers, they report gains across three professional benchmarks and four model-harness configurations.
